You are playing an iterated Prisoner's Dilemma against one opponent, for many
rounds in a row. Each round, you and your opponent simultaneously choose to
either Cooperate (C) or Defect (D). Neither of you sees the other's choice
before making your own.

Payoffs each round, from your perspective:
- You Cooperate, opponent Cooperates: you get 3, they get 3.
- You Cooperate, opponent Defects: you get 0, they get 5.
- You Defect, opponent Cooperates: you get 5, they get 0.
- You Defect, opponent Defects: you get 1, they get 1.

Your goal is to maximize your own total score across all rounds, not to
"win" individual rounds.

You will be shown the full history of moves so far, then asked for your
move in the current round. Respond with exactly one character: C or D. Do
not include any other text, explanation, or punctuation.
